## 🎛️ Customizing Your Experience (Optional Fun)

>

If you want to tweak how hinge behaves, click the little hinge icon in your menu bar. A small dropdown menu appears with these options:

>

- **Sensitivity** - This controls how much your wallpaper moves when you tilt the lid. Set it low for a subtle effect, or crank it high for a dramatic whoosh. Default is medium, which feels just right.
.
- **Animation Speed** - How quickly your wallpaper snaps to the new position. Set it to "Instant" for a snappy feel, or "Smooth" for a dreamy glide. Auto is recommended (it's smart!).)
- **Wallpaper Style** - Choose from a few built-in scenic wallpapers that look great in 3D, or select "Use My Photos" to use any picture from your photo library. (Pro tip: close-up nature shots and city skylines work best!).)
- **Launch at Login** - Toggle this ON if you want hinge to start automatically every time you turn on your MacBook. Super convenient if your like the effect all the time.
.
- **Pause hinge** - Need to screenshot or present? Pause it temporarily without quitting. Click again to resume.

## 🧰 Troubleshooting Tips (When Things Get Weird)



**Problem: The hinge icon doesn't appear in the menu bar.**
*Solution:* Go to your Applications folder, right-click hinge, and select "Open". macOS might ask you to confirm - click "Open" again. Once it runs once,your'll never have this issue again.



**Problem: The wallpaper doesn't move when I tilt my lid.**
*Solution:* Make sure you're not in "Low Power Mode" (System Settings > Battery > Low Power Modeey). Also, try toggling "Sensitivity" up in the hinge menu. If still nothing, restart hinge (quit from menu bar, then reopen from Applications).)



**Problem: My Mac says"hinge" is damaged and can't be opened."**
*Solution:* This is rare,but happens with some downloaded apps. Right-click hinge, select "Open" (not double-click), and choose "Open" in the popup. macOS will remember your choice next time. You can also check your Gatekeeper settings (System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down to "Allow apps from").)



**Problem: The effect seems reversed (opening lid moves wallpaper the wrong waye()).**
*Solution:* In the hinge menu, look for "Lid Direction" (it's under Sensitive settings). Toggle it. Instant fix.
